# A loading screen with a little too much personality 🧬🍄

I built TwistyTrippy with AI because apparently a spinning circle wasn’t dramatic enough. Glowing DNA, dancing letters, and a mushroom mode that gets a little carried away.

The video is the fun part. The whole project is open source—including the playground to mess with it yourself.
